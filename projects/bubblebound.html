Gameplay

What it is

Bubblebound is a cozy puzzle adventure that lets players explore an underwater world as a fish, meet sea creatures, and progress by solving puzzles.

My role

Personal ownership, shared work, and supporting evidence.

Collaborative game design

I co-developed the core loop, controls, movement, and puzzles with the design team.

Art coordination

I worked with the art lead on visual consistency and organized an asset-sorting sheet for the 13-person team.

What I designed

The systems, decisions, and feedback that shape the player experience.

Elemental puzzle ideas

The team’s planning notes explore fire melting ice walls, ice freezing moving objects, and earth creating a crossing over an electrified wire. These are documented design ideas, not a claim that every mechanic shipped.

Turning a world into an asset list

The production list separates fish, wall types, backgrounds, rocks, and electrical hazards. It records the objects the art and design teams needed to coordinate.
