What it is

Chronomancy is a single-player, magic-driven dungeon crawler. Players explore randomly generated rooms and cast spells by tracing a shape mid-combat.

New spells and stronger items build toward a showdown with the Chronomancer. My contribution focused on models, rigs, and animation; the overall game and spellcasting system were team work.

My role

Personal ownership, shared work, and supporting evidence.

Designed

I designed idle, walk, move, attack, and stagger animations for enemy variants.

Implemented

I built the first-person hand model and control rigs, plus rigs for melee and flying enemies. I authored the in-game animations, including casting gestures, kicks, and enemy movement.

Collaborated

Animator on an 11-person team. I do not claim ownership of the drawing-recognition system, procedural rooms, or overall game design.
