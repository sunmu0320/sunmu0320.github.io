What it is

A solo-developed combat demo set in a mysterious environment, built with the goal of making melee combat fun and satisfying to play.

My role

Personal ownership, shared work, and supporting evidence.

Combat implementation

I built animation-notify-driven weapon tracing and integrated melee animations, including sword sheathe and unsheathe states.

Enemy behavior & feedback

I implemented patrol and chase behavior and authored weapon-trail VFX in this solo Unreal Engine prototype.

What I designed

The systems, decisions, and feedback that shape the player experience.

Weapon Tracing

Animation notifies drive collision detection during weapon movement.

Enemy AI

Enemies follow defined patrol tracks until spotting the player, then give chase.

Animation & VFX

Sword sheathe and unsheathe states combine with weapon-trail VFX for readable combat.
